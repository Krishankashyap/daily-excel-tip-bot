## SUM - din ki total entry

```
=SUM(C2:C100)
```

DEO ki aam zindagi: din bhar ki sales, attendance ya amount ka total. C2:C100 me jo bhi numbers hain, ek formula se total — haath se jodne ki galti khatam.
