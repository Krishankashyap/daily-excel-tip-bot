## IFERROR - error wali sheet saaf rakho

```
=IFERROR(A2/B2, 0)
```

Divide by zero ya missing data par #DIV/0! ki jagah 0 dikhega. Boss ko bhejne wali sheet me error nahi dikhna chahiye.
