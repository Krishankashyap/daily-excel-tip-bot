## SUMIFS - mahina + sheher ka total

```
=SUMIFS(C2:C100, A2:A100, "Delhi", B2:B100, "Nov")
```

November + Delhi dono shart poori hone wali rows ka total. Monthly report banane me roz kaam aata hai.
