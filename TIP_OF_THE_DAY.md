## Advance: SUMPRODUCT - bina helper column ke total

```
=SUMPRODUCT((A2:A100="Delhi")*(C2:C100))
```

Bina extra column banaye, shart wali rows ka total. Advance level ka formula, par DEO report me bahut kaam aata hai.
